\paragraph{Where intervention time comes from.}
The 6,485,664 filter-on steps contain 480,283 intervention events. Although
60.47\% last one step, the 0.98\% lasting at least 100 steps contribute
76.22\% of all intervention steps. Every one of the 2,972,091 uncertified
steps is also a recovery step, accounting for 85.41\% of recovery time.
Thus the primary efficiency bottleneck is long dwell in uncertified recovery,
not merely frequent one-step clipping; Fig.~S1 in the supplement gives the
complete event decomposition.
